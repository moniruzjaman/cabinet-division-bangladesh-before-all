01 · THE CRISIS

The warehouse is full. The farmer's hand is not.

Bangladesh spends roughly Tk 17,001 crore a year on fertilizer subsidy — close to 80% of public agricultural spending — yet the bottom 40% of farmers receive only about 15% of the benefit. Between the warehouse and the farmer stands a layer of leakage.

Price distortion

+33% to +100% premiums over MRP

A 50 kg TSP bag priced officially at Tk 1,350 sells at Tk 1,700–1,800 in Rajshahi; TSP per-kg doubles from ~Tk 26 to Tk 52 in Jashore. In Kurigram Sadar, only one-third of August 2025 urea allocation was lifted within season — an artificial-scarcity signal.

Field evidenceDAE · PARTNER 2023–26
Agronomic failure

95.2% over-apply urea

Among surveyed Kurigram mustard farmers, 95.2% apply urea above the recommended dose, 94.8% over-apply TSP and 89.6% over-apply Muriate of Potash. Only 9.2% consult a specialist; 56.8% decide dose by personal experience.

DiagnosisGuesswork, not science
Institutional

33–45% dealer lifting

Dealers lift only 33–45% of allocation within season; unrecorded sales, black-market premiums, diversion to neighbouring markets, and subsidy leakage compound. Allocation is negotiated through dealers, not tied to land and crop.

Root causeNo verification at the last mile
Why Kurigram first

537,543 t food surplus · 382 chars

Kurigram turned monga into surplus, scaled BRRI dhan 100+ 36× in two years, runs 12,350 ha under AWD, and exports mango — yet carries the hardest geography: 16 rivers, 382 chars, floods that rise 50 cm in 24 hours. If reform works here, it works anywhere.

Evidence basePARTNER 2023–26 · DAE GPMS
02 · ARCHITECTURE

Four layers, one grid, one package, one char promise

The proposed journey replaces Import/BADC → Dealer → Farmer (validated informally) with Import/BADC → local server + biometric PoS → farmer's hands. Subsidy releases only on a verified sale; every kilogram is traced on a permissioned blockchain ledger.

Layer 1

Identity & Land — the Farmer Card

100% enrolment of 566,202 farm families on the Krishak Card, biometrically linked to National ID and to BDS/EDLMS land parcels via Union Parishad single-window desks. 120 data points per farmer; GPMS baseline 0.02 lakh → 5.66 lakh.

Target: 566,202 families · linked to NID + parcels
Layer 2

Point of Sale — fingerprint match, offline-first

116 biometric PoS terminals across 107 outlets (76 union/paurashava dealers + 31 char hubs) with 9 spares. 9 upazila local-server nodes + district mirror so char sales never stop for a network outage. Bangla voice UI.

Target: 116 PoS · 9+1 servers · uptime ≥ 98%
Layer 3

Tracking & Transparency — the real-time audit

Central Digital Ledger with permissioned blockchain traces every kilogram import → warehouse → dealer → farmer. Public dashboard, automated leakage alerts, quarterly public reports to the Manifesto Implementation Committee.

Target: 95% sales authenticated · leakage < 5%
Layer 4

Advisory — the Soil Health Grid

Not blanket cards. A land-based grid: 5,676 plain samples (1/20 ha) + 4,403 char samples (1/10 ha) + 1,008 QC = 11,087 lab tests generating 436,902 parcel-linked digital Soil Health Cards. SRDI full-profile @ Tk 1,500; digital card @ Tk 40.

Target: 436,902 digital SHCs · N reduction −30%
Cross-cutting instruments

Modified One-Must-One-Should — adapted from Vietnam's “One Must, Five Reductions” (−31.3% fertilizer use): certified seed + soil-test-based entitlement as the Must; five crop-specific reductions as the Should. Delivered through 1,314 block demonstrations, 56,620 lead farmers (≥50% women), 187 SAAOs retrained.

The char promise — 107 solar irrigation pumps (ending the Rabi water crisis), 9 speedboats, 9 fair-price collection centres, 100,000 char saplings, BRRI dhan 111/114 community seed for flood-prone blocks.

Returns

In steady state (Year 4+), the project recovers or saves ≈ Tk 78.9 crore/yr (leakage recovery Tk 36.0 Cr · input-cost saving Tk 32.4 Cr · post-harvest loss cut Tk 9.1 Cr · diesel displaced Tk 1.4 Cr). 06 · THE DIGITAL GOVERNANCE STACK

The integrity layer is not on the critical path of new development.

The companion e-bill system repository carries a Prisma data model already purpose-built for fertilizer subsidy governance. The Central Digital Ledger, public dashboard, leakage alerts and QR receipts correspond to fields that already exist in working code.

Beneficiary registry

User · National ID hash · KYC

Roles FARMER / DEALER / ADMIN; KYC status PENDING / VERIFIED / REJECTED; verification level 1–3; land-title and cooperative-cert IDs. Maps cleanly to the Krishak Card beneficiary master.

Last-mile integrity

Dealer · geofencing · licence

Unique licence number, shop name, district/upazila, latitude, longitude, service radius in km. Geo-fenced sale validation at the point of sale — exactly the “last-mile integrity” mechanism the briefings specify.

Subsidy math

Fertilizer · subsidy % · season cap

Type (UREA/TSP/DAP/MOP/GYPSUM/ZINC_SULPHATE/COMPOUND/ORGANIC), N-P-K composition, price per unit, subsidy rate %, max per farmer per season. Per-season entitlement caps are native.

Tamper-evident ledger

Transaction · SHA-256 hash · QR

Farmer↔dealer, transactionHash for blockchain, geofence-verified lat/long, subsidy amount, farmer payment, blockchainRecorded flag, qrCodeReference, status. The subsidy-disbursement ledger with QR receipt.

Recommendation engine

SoilHealthCard · N/P/K/pH/OM

N, P, K, pH and organic-matter levels, recommendedFertilizers JSON, district/upazila/union. Maps to the 12-parameter soil-testing reference and the parcel-linked digital card.

Accountability trail

AuditLog · who/what/when/IP

Action, entity type, entity ID, user ID, oldValues/newValues JSON, IP address. Cabinet-grade accountability trail required for the reform's accountability narrative.

08 · GOVERNANCE & OVERSIGHT

Five tiers. No parallel bureaucracy.

The project deliberately avoids creating a parallel bureaucracy. Existing DAE offices host field coordinators; existing Union Parishad desks host single-window service; existing SRDI labs host the soil grid. The Cabinet Division's role is convening and accountability.

Tier 1

Steering Committee

Chaired by the Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture; meets quarterly; approves gate reviews. Members: Finance Division, DG DAE, Manifesto Implementation Committee liaison, Cabinet Division observer.

Tier 2

Project Management Unit

At DAE Kurigram, Deputy Director as Project Director. 14 staff (PD, 2 deputies, 2 M&E, 3 IT, 2 accounts, 4 field coordinators) embedded in existing DAE offices.

Tier 3

Union Parishad single-window

Farmer Card enrolment, Soil Health Card download, grievance intake and e-voucher assistance at UP desks. Local problems resolved locally, per the manifesto.

Tier 4

Oversight Committee

Farmer leaders, civil society, UP chairs, technical experts. Quarterly public transparency reports feeding the Manifesto Implementation Committee's public accountability assessments.

Tier 5

Technical partners & audit regime

SRDI (labs), BARI/BRRI (variety pipeline), BADC (dealer enforcement under 2026 Policy), a2i & ICT Division (cloud and UID-grade reader standards). Three-layer audit: real-time ledger audit (every payment event), annual C&AG audit, and an annual independent performance audit (component C11, outcome-based).
