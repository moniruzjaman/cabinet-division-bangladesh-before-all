APart A — Project Summary প্রকল্প সারাংশ

1.Project Title প্রকল্পের নাম

Kurigram Integrated Fertilizer Governance & Farmer Card Pilot (KIFGP) — biometric point-of-sale fertilizer distribution, a land-based Soil Health Grid, a Modified One-Must-One-Should extension package, and char resilience & market infrastructure across Kurigram district. The pilot converts a Tk 17,001 crore national fertilizer subsidy from a channel of leakage into a verified, auditable, farmer-facing system built on the nation's own digital rails.

2.Sponsoring Ministry / Division / Implementing Agency বাস্তবায়নকারী সংস্থা

Ministry / DivisionMinistry of Agriculture (MoA) Implementing agencyDepartment of Agricultural Extension (DAE), Kurigram Co-implementersBADC (supply & dealer network), SRDI (soil grid), BARI/BRRI (varieties), Union Parishads & LGD (single-window desks), ICT Division (ledger hosting on national cloud / local nodes) SteeringSecretary, MoA (Chair); Finance Division representative; Director General DAE; Manifesto Implementation Committee liaison

3.Project Location প্রকল্প এলাকা

Kurigram district: 9 upazilas, 73 unions, 3 paurashavas, 219 extension blocks, 382 chars (107 char blocks; 54,720 ha char area; 44,027 ha cultivable). AEZs 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 22. Pilot outlets: 76 union/paurashava dealer points (per the 2026 Dealer Policy: one dealer per union) + 31 char hub sub-depots = 107 outlets.

4.Project Duration মেয়াদ

36 months (01 July 2026 – 30 June 2029), aligned with the DAE Kurigram GPMS three-year plan 2026-29 and the PARTNER programme cycle. Two decision gates at end of Year 1 and end of Year 2 (see §18).

6.Objectives উদ্দেশ্যসমূহ

  1. Eliminate subsidy leakage and price distortion through fingerprint-match authentication at every fertilizer sale (PoS + upazila local server), with crop-declaration and land-parcel validation.
  2. Complete the Farmer Card in Kurigram: 100% enrolment of 566,202 farm families (508,493 families + 57,709 sharecroppers) on the Krishak Card, linked to NID and BDS/EDLMS parcels.
  3. Replace blanket soil-card targets with a land-based Soil Health Grid: 11,087 composite tests generating 436,902 parcel-linked digital Soil Health Cards.
  4. Raise nutrient use efficiency and farmer income through the Modified One-Must-One-Should package (certified seed + soil-test-based entitlement; five crop-specific reductions).
  5. Secure char agriculture: solar irrigation, extension access by water, fair-price collection centres, stress-tolerant variety seed systems.
  6. Institutionalize real-time and performance audit, open tendering, union-level single-window service and independent oversight per the 31-Point State Reform Plan and July National Charter.

9.Priority Justification for the Finance Division যৌক্তিকতা

The district fertilizer flow is ≈120,000 t/yr, carrying ≈Tk 360 crore of annual subsidy. Current leakage, premiums (+33% to +100% observed in comparable districts) and over-application (95.2% of surveyed Kurigram mustard farmers above urea recommendation) erode that flow every season. The one-time Tk 3,949.6 lakh outlay equals 11% of a single year's district subsidy flow, Tk 697 per farm family over three years, and Tk 112 per bigha per year — against documented savings of Tk 1,052.35 per bigha per year from corrected fertilizer use alone. Approval is justified as manifesto-mandated public investment; the economics (§21) confirm, not condition, that judgment.

BPart B — Project Details বিস্তারিত প্রকল্প প্রস্তাবনা

10.Background & Rationale পটভূমি

Existing journey. Import / BADC stores → dealer network → farmers, validated informally by SAAO recommendation, familiar face, NID or paper card. Result: unrecorded sales, artificial scarcity (Kurigram Sadar lifted one-third of allocated urea in Aug 2025), black-market premiums, and a subsidy benefit distribution in which the bottom 40% of farmers receive ~15% (World Bank).

Proposed journey. Import / BADC → local server + biometric PoS → farmer's hands. Authentication by fingerprint match (PoS + upazila local server) plus crop declaration and land-parcel validation against BDS/EDLMS. Subsidy releases only on a verified sale (India DBT-PoS model: 287,000 retailers, 84 crore farmers, no-denial since 2018). Every kilogram is traceable on a permissioned blockchain ledger with a public dashboard.

Why Kurigram first. The district is a surplus food producer (537,543 MT in 2024-25) with proven absorptive capacity: BRRI dhan 100+ scaled 36× (aman 90 → 3,289 ha), boro 6,362 → 20,350 ha in two years; AWD on 12,350 ha saving 30% irrigation water; 51 Farmer Service Centres holding Tk 12,40,000; 50 t mango exported; 959 rabi demonstrations delivered at 100% in 2025-26. It also concentrates the hardest cases — 382 chars, flash floods rising 50 cm in 24 hours — so a system that works here works anywhere.

11.Problem & Needs Analysis সমস্যা বিশ্লেষণ

  • Fiscal: subsidy leakage and diversion; dealer lifting 33–45% of allocation within season; premiums over MRP.
  • Equity: bottom-40% share of benefit ~15%; top quintile ~50%.
  • Agronomic: dose by personal experience (56.8%); only 9.2% consult specialists; urea/TSP/MP overuse 95.2 / 94.8 / 89.6%.
  • Char-specific: Rabi irrigation crisis; demonstration input allocation below market price (DAE-identified constraint); costly access; no fair-price collection points; soil fertility decline on sandy chars.
  • Institutional: FSC savings locked by legal/institutional complexity; women lack field facilities (toilets, transport, day-time scheduling); data gaps in Bhurungamari & Roumari reporting.

12.Climate Change & Disaster Risk Screening জলবায়ু ও দুর্যোগ ঝুঁকি

Per IMED climate-mainstreaming requirements: the project area lies in a flood-pulse hydrological sweet spot of 16 rivers (Brahmaputra, Dharla, Teesta, Dudhkumar). Adaptation and mitigation measures embedded:

  • 107 solar irrigation pumps replacing diesel in off-grid char blocks (mitigation + price-shock protection).
  • AWD expansion 12,350 → 25,000 ha (−30% water, −methane).
  • BRRI dhan 111/114 and stress-tolerant community seed for flood-prone blocks (111 validated: 4.2 t/ha, survives shallow submergence; 8 t seed banked; 600 ha pipeline).
  • 100,000 char saplings for bank stabilization; collection centres sited above flood level with raised plinths.
  • Speedboats and offline-first PoS as disaster-continuity measures for service delivery.

13.Environmental & Social Safeguards, Gender & Inclusion পরিবেশ ও সামাজিক সুরক্ষা

  • Gender: ≥50% of 56,620 lead farmers women (baseline female PFS share 28.3–39%; 2025-26 PFS women 800 vs men 675); women-only batches with local venues and convenient timing; female-headed households first in e-voucher queue; Union Women Support Cell linkage.
  • Inclusion: sharecroppers (57,709) and landless livestock/fishery families enrolled on Farmer Card per manifesto extension; USSD/SMS Bangla advisory for non-smartphone majority; voice UI for low literacy.
  • Environment: reduced nitrous-oxide and nitrate runoff from SHC-dosed entitlements; no civil works in river channels; solar assets e-waste clause in tender.
  • Grievance: union-level cells + national portal; disposal target 98% (GPMS baseline 90%).

16.Implementation Arrangement বাস্তবায়ন কৌশল

  • Steering Committee (Secretary MoA chair) — quarterly; approves gate reviews.
  • PMU at DAE Kurigram, Deputy Director as Project Director; upazila field coordinators embedded in existing DAE offices (no parallel bureaucracy).
  • Union Parishad single-window desks for Farmer Card, SHC download, grievance intake and e-voucher assistance — "local problems resolved locally" per manifesto.

  • Technical partners: SRDI (labs), BARI/BRRI (variety pipeline incl. ≤70-day mustard, canola, blast-resistant wheat), BADC (dealer enforcement under 2026 Policy), a2i/ICT (cloud & UID-grade reader standards).

18.Financial Management, Disbursement & Gates আর্থিক ব্যবস্থাপনা

  • Funds flow: ADP release → PMU designated account (iBAS/CGA rails); partner co-finance via programme account; in-kind logged at framework rates.

  • Real-time audit: every payment event on ledger; monthly reconciliation with BADC MIS; quarterly unaudited financial reports to Finance Division; annual audit by C&AG plus independent performance audit (C11).

22.Sustainability & Exit Strategy স্থায়িত্ব

  • PoS & server O&M: covered in C2 for 2 years, then absorbed into DAE revenue budget and dealer licence fees under the 2026 Policy.
  • Ledger & dashboard: transferred to MoA ICT wing; source code and data sovereign on national infrastructure.
  • Solar SIPs: char-block water-user groups with volumetric tariff for maintenance reserve.
  • Collection centres: operated by converted FSC cooperatives/SMEs (51 FSCs → 100+), resolving the legal-institutional barrier identified by PARTNER.
  • Extension practice: lead-farmer network and SHC-dose advisories continue within GPMS business-as-usual targets.
